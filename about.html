Bay Breeze Heating & Air is a family-owned heating and cooling company based in Tampa, Florida. We keep it small on purpose, so the person who quotes your job is close to the person who does the work.

Our story

Bay Breeze started in 2012 with one truck, a borrowed ladder, and a simple rule: treat every home like it is on your own street. Tampa summers do not forgive a broken air conditioner, and we saw too many neighbors waiting days for a callback from companies that were too big to care.

Fourteen years on, we run a small crew of licensed technicians across the Tampa Bay area. We still quote most repairs from photos first, because it respects your time: you send us what the system is doing, we tell you honestly whether it is a repair, a tune-up, or time to replace, and we put the price in writing before anyone picks up a wrench.

What we will never do: sell you a new system when a $200 part fixes the old one, surprise you with fees at the door, or leave a job without showing you the old parts and explaining what failed.
